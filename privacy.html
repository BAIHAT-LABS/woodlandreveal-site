Privacy Policy — Woodland Reveal

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Woodland Reveal (“Woodland Reveal” or “the App”) handles information. By using Woodland Reveal, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

Woodland Reveal is a mobile puzzle game published by BAIHAT LABS. For privacy requests about Woodland Reveal, contact WoodlandReveal@baihatlabs.com.

2. Information we collect

Woodland Reveal has no accounts and does not send your game data to our servers. The information involved is:

  • Game progress and settings. Solved levels, stars, hints, album pieces, owned wood themes, language, sound and other preferences are stored locally on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Purchase information. When you buy In-App Purchases in Woodland Reveal, Apple or Google processes payment and provides the App with enough information to deliver the purchased item (for example, product identifier and transaction status). We do not receive your name, email address or payment card number.
  • Advertising data. Woodland Reveal shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded video ads, which play only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward such as a hint, and interstitial ads, which may appear between levels (never during the first levels, and no more often than every few levels). To deliver, measure and limit the frequency of these ads, Google may collect device information, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic data. On iOS, the device advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you tap Allow in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise ads are shown without it. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses data from partner apps).
  • Support communications. If you email us about Woodland Reveal, we receive the content of your message and any details you include (such as device type, app version or screenshots) so we can respond.

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Provide Woodland Reveal gameplay and keep your saved progress on your device;
  • Process and deliver In-App Purchases, and restore non-consumable purchases;
  • Show advertisements and grant in-game rewards when you choose to watch a rewarded ad;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive the advertising data Google collects.

4. Sharing of information

We may share limited information related to Woodland Reveal with:

  • Google AdMob — to serve ads and honor your tracking choice;
  • Apple — App Store purchases and related services;
  • Google — Google Play Billing on Android;
  • Hosting providers — to serve this website;
  • Authorities — when required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local Woodland Reveal game data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Purchase records are retained by Apple or Google according to their policies; Remove Ads, wood themes and bundles can be restored with Restore Purchases in Settings after reinstalling. Support emails are retained as needed to resolve your request and for legitimate business or legal purposes, then deleted when no longer needed. You may ask us to delete your support emails at any time.

6. Children

Woodland Reveal is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use HTTPS for this website and rely on Apple’s and Google’s secure purchase systems for store transactions related to Woodland Reveal. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe there has been unauthorized access related to the App.

8. Your choices

  • You may erase all Woodland Reveal data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data.
  • You may manage or request refunds for store purchases through Apple or Google.
  • On iOS you can allow or stop tracking at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. The Remove Ads purchase turns off interstitial ads; rewarded ads stay available for when you want a free reward.
  • You may contact us about Woodland Reveal privacy or support emails: WoodlandReveal@baihatlabs.com.

9. International processing

Your device stores local Woodland Reveal game data. Apple and Google may process payment and advertising data in multiple countries. This website is hosted on servers that may be outside your country of residence.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Woodland Reveal after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
