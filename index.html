Woodland Reveal

Cozy wood sliding puzzles & nature art

Woodland Reveal is a relaxing sliding block puzzle. Slide the wooden blocks to clear a path for the golden star block, and every solved level reveals a piece of a hand-painted woodland scene for your album.

Support Privacy Policy

Gameplay

Hundreds of handcrafted levels across woodland chapters, a daily puzzle and an endless mode. No account is needed: your progress, album and settings are stored on your device.

Ads and In-App Purchases

Woodland Reveal is free to play and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Hints, extra wood themes, a starter bundle and Remove Ads are sold as In-App Purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
